I am a PhD student in the Theoretical Materials Chemistry group at Uppsala University. My research combines molecular simulations, multiscale modelling, and machine learning to study transport phenomena in functional materials.

My current work focuses on understanding diffusion in solid-state electrolytes for next-generation energy storage and investigating gas diffusion and adsorption in nanoporous materials. By combining atomistic simulations with data-driven approaches, I aim to uncover the fundamental mechanisms governing material performance and to accelerate the discovery and design of new materials.

This website contains information about my research, publications, software projects, and academic activities.

Research Interest
======
- Computational Chemistry
- Molecular Dynamics
- Statistical Thermodynamics
- Physical Chemistry
- Organic Chemistry
- Machine Learning

Open-source Projects

More information about these and other projects can be found in the [Portfolio](https://matevzturk.github.io/portfolio) section. Details of my research output are available in the [Publications](https://matevzturk.github.io/publications) section.
